Shri Chintamani Hanuman Temple

12-Foot Monolithic Black Granite Idol • 20-Foot Elevated Hillock

The heart and pride of Vidit Signature is the magnificent Chintamani Hanuman Mandir. Carved meticulously out of a single continuous block of black granite stone, the 12-foot murti radiates immense divine grace, courage, and tranquility.

Elevated upon a specially designed 20-foot hillock, the temple commands panoramic 360-degree vistas of the 25-acre township. Surrounded by lush flowering gardens, peaceful water cascade elements, and broad marble walkways, it serves as a daily sanctuary for prayer, meditation, and inner stillness.

Dedicated In-Township Goshala (Cow Sanctuary)

In ancient Indian town planning, a Goshala was the epicenter of purity, abundance, and organic balance. Vidit Signature revitalizes this heritage with an on-site, hygienically managed cow sanctuary nestled within the green peripheral foliage of the township.

Residents and children enjoy direct access to Go-Seva, morning feeding rituals, and fresh, organic atmosphere. The Goshala is maintained by dedicated staff with veterinary supervision and natural green fodder grown right within the township.

  • Pure indigenous Gir and Sahiwal cows maintained in peaceful shelter
  • Zero foul odors or disturbance due to advanced scientific bio-composting
  • Nurtures compassionate cultural values for the next generation

Buyer Clarifications

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about township living, maintenance, and approvals at Vidit Signature.

Is the project RERA approved and legally clear?
Yes, Vidit Signature is 100% RERA registered with clear titles, government land diversion approvals, and sanctioned layout plans. Every buyer receives immediate Tehsil registry support and Namantaran mutation assistance.
Can non-residents or public visit the Chintamani Hanuman Mandir?
The Chintamani Hanuman Mandir is dedicated to the township and community devotees. Dedicated parking and access pathways have been organized so township residents enjoy privacy and security while devotees can offer prayers peacefully.
What are the plot sizes available, and what can I build?
Plot configurations range from 410 sq. ft. for compact smart houses to 2,266 sq. ft. for grand villas. Popular sizes include 600, 800, 1000, 1200, 1500, 1800, and 2266 sq. ft. You are free to construct custom single-floor, duplex (G+1), or triplex (G+2) residential homes as per municipal norms.
Are home loans approved on Vidit Signature plots?
Yes, leading nationalized and private banks including State Bank of India (SBI), HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, and Bank of Baroda have approved the project for plot purchase and composite plot+construction loans up to 80% financing.
How are township common amenities and security maintained?
Vidit Builders manages a dedicated on-site facility management team that handles round-the-clock security guards, CCTV operation, road street lighting, landscape gardening, park upkeep, and Goshala maintenance.
