Vidit Signature is purposefully designed around peace, devotion, and mindful community living. The township houses a divine spiritual sanctuary elevated above the landscape, harmonizing modern architectural grandeur with ancient Vedic serenity.

Centering the spiritual energy of the township is the grand Chintamani Hanuman Temple. It proudly features a magnificent 12-foot single black granite stone idol of Lord Hanuman, perched majestically atop a specially crafted 20-foot elevated hill.
